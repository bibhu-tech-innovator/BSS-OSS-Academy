About BSS/OSS Academy

BSS/OSS Academy exists because transformation programmes fail when people don’t share a common understanding of the architecture they’re building. Too often, critical concepts like catalog-driven design, CFS/RFS decomposition, and source-of-record ownership are understood differently — or not at all — across business, IT, and vendor teams.

This platform was built to close that gap: a single, vendor-neutral reference that creates shared understanding and supports better decisions across every level of a telco organisation.

How Organisations Use This Platform

•

Onboarding new staff into BSS/OSS and Lead-to-Cash concepts

Get people productive faster by giving them a structured path through the architecture, rather than relying on tribal knowledge.

•

Aligning business, IT, and transformation teams around a common language

Reduce miscommunication by ensuring everyone uses the same terms with the same meaning.

•

Supporting large-scale BSS/OSS and Lead-to-Cash transformation programmes

Provide programme teams with a consistent architectural baseline that doesn’t depend on any single vendor’s framing.

•

Acting as a standard glossary of terms and architectural concepts

Settle definitional debates quickly by referencing TM Forum–aligned definitions that the whole organisation can point to.

•

Enabling internal certification to prove baseline competence

Use the built-in assessments and quizzes to verify that staff understand the fundamentals before they join critical workstreams.

•

Creating a shared transformation baseline across programmes and vendors

Ensure that internal teams, system integrators, and product vendors are all working from the same architectural assumptions.

•

Supporting executive and steering-committee decision-making

Give senior leaders enough architectural context to challenge proposals, evaluate trade-offs, and ask the right questions.

•

Using the platform as an interview and assessment tool

Evaluate candidates’ reasoning about BSS/OSS architecture, not just their familiarity with specific products.

What This Platform Is (and Is Not)

This platform is

  • A reference and alignment tool for telco architecture
  • A practical guide to transformation thinking
  • A shared baseline for complex programmes

This platform is not

  • A vendor training site
  • A certification authority
  • A replacement for hands-on implementation experience

BSS/OSS Academy exists to ensure everyone involved in transformation is solving the same problem — with the same understanding.
