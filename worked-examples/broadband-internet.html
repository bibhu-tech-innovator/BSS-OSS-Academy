Domain Examples/Broadband Internet (FTTH)

Broadband Internet (FTTH)

End-to-end flow for activating a residential FTTH broadband service, from online address serviceability check through order capture with appointment booking, truck roll installation, OLT/ONT activation, speed profile application, billing start, and early-life line monitoring.

Architecture Overview

Residential FTTH delivers broadband over a GPON/XGS-PON fibre network. An ONT at the customer premises connects to the OLT in the exchange via passive splitters. The BNG/BRAS terminates subscriber sessions and applies speed profiles and QoS.

Customer Premises

Home devices connect via Wi-Fi router to the ONT. The ONT terminates the fibre and provides Ethernet/Wi-Fi to the home.

ONT (CPE)

Optical Network Terminal installed at the premises. Operator-supplied, configured via TR-069/ACS. Bridges fibre to Ethernet.

PON Access Network

GPON/XGS-PON with passive splitters (1:32/1:64). Shared downstream bandwidth, TDMA upstream. OLT at the exchange headend.

BNG & Core

BNG/BRAS terminates PPPoE/IPoE sessions, applies speed profiles via RADIUS, and routes to the internet. AAA authenticates subscribers.

Services & Internet

DNS, DHCP, TR-069 ACS for ONT management, billing/mediation, and internet peering for upstream connectivity.
