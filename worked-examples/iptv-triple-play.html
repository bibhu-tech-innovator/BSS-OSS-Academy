Domain Examples/IPTV / Triple-Play Bundle

IPTV / Triple-Play Bundle

End-to-end flow for activating a residential triple-play bundle (1 Gbps FTTH + VoIP + Premium IPTV with 2 STBs), from serviceability through multi-CFS decomposition, parallel broadband/voice/IPTV activation, STB provisioning with CAS pairing, middleware entitlement, bundle billing, and correlated multi-service assurance.

Architecture Overview

Triple-play bundles three services over a single FTTH access connection: broadband (data VLAN), VoIP (voice VLAN), and IPTV (multicast VLAN). Each CFS has independent provisioning but shares the physical OLT/ONT layer. IPTV adds middleware, CAS, and STB management complexity.

Customer Premises

ONT provides Ethernet for data, FXS for voice, and dedicated port for STBs. 2× STBs (4K + HD) receive multicast IPTV.

ONT + STBs (CPE)

ONT bridges fibre to triple-play VLANs. STBs are operator-supplied managed devices with CAS pairing and TR-069 management.

PON + Triple-Play VLANs

GPON access with separate VLANs: 100 (data), 200 (voice), 300 (IPTV multicast), 400 (management). H-QoS per service class.

Service Platforms

BNG for broadband, IMS/softswitch for voice, IPTV middleware + CAS + DRM for TV. Each CFS has independent activation.

Content & Interconnect

IPTV headend/CDN for live and VOD content. PSTN/mobile interconnect for voice. EPG and content rights management.
