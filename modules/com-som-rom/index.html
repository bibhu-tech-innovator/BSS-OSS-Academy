Order Management (COM/SOM/ROM)

The Order Orchestration Chain

How commercial orders decompose into service orders and resource orders, with orchestration at every layer. This module covers the full lifecycle from order capture through decomposition, service design, resource allocation, and activation.

10 sections Estimated reading: 3 hours Process domain

Module Overview

Order management sits at the heart of every telecom operator's BSS/OSS estate. It is the bridge between what a customer buys (the commercial order) and what the network delivers (activated services on provisioned resources). In modern, catalog-driven architectures, this bridge is built on a three-layer order model that decomposes a single commercial order into dedicated service and resource orders.

This module provides a deep, vendor-neutral exploration of the COM/SOM/ROM model. You will learn how commercial orders are captured, validated, and decomposed; how service orders design and manage the customer-facing and resource-facing services; and how resource orders drive activation and configuration across the network domains.

Prerequisites: It is strongly recommended that you complete Module 1 (Foundations) and Module 2 (Catalog Modeling Deep Dive) before starting this module. The three-layer catalog model (Product / Service / Resource) is the foundation on which COM/SOM/ROM operates.
